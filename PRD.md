# PRD - Disney Character Encyclopedia

## One-sentence pitch

An encyclopedia to explore the world of disney characters, Including their allies and enemies

## Who is it for?

Disney fans, curious kids and anyone else who wants to learn about their favorite disney character

## Screens

1. Character List: list every character with it's name and picture
1. Character details: Display movie appearances, tv show appearances, and allies
1. Characters in movie: list characters appearing in certain movie
1. Characters in tv shows: lisr character appearing in certain tv show

## Must-Have features

1. Show list of characters
1. Filter the list of characters
1. Click a Character to show its details
1. Click a movie inside a character to filter characters by movie
1. Click a tv show inside a character to filter characters by shows


## Acceptance criteria

- When I open the app, I see a list of disney characters
- Before I click anything, I see "Pick a character"
- When I click a character, I see their movie appearances, tv show appearances, and allies
- When inside a character, if I click on a name of a movie, I see characters filtered by that movie
- When inside a character, if I click on a name of a tv show, I see characters filtered by that show

## Data

DisneyAPI `https://api.disneyapi.dev/character`. an api that allows to query characters
